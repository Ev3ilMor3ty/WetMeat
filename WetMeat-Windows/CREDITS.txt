WetMeat 1.0 Stable
=================

Stockfish project: https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish
Upstream source: 49ea5ded38315cff8e67f4a677a9e7811612fbf6, 30 September 2026.
Official reference opponent used in the studies: Stockfish 19.
Model: nn-252f33942263.nnue, unchanged official Stockfish weights.

Stockfish's authors supply the chess search, evaluation and neural-network
weights. Their notices remain in the source, AUTHORS and Copying.txt.

WetMeat's project changes include the UCI stop/ponder correction, a Windows
CPU-topology fallback, release builds, test infrastructure and documentation.
The name change does not make the Stockfish core an independent implementation.

The Stockfish-derived engine is GPL v3 or later. The source collection also
includes independent prototypes and research tools, with their own licence
files preserved. Those projects are clearly separated from the stable release.

The supplied executables are the previously verified stable binaries. This
distribution refresh changes the English documentation and delivery helpers,
not the engine algorithms or weights. Measured match results belong to the
specific binary and settings identified in the test notes.
